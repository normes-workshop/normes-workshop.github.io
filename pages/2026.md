Created four years ago, the NORMES group (successor to a previous multidisciplinary research collective, LIGC, focused on logic, computer science, philosophy and their interactions with other fields) has aimed to foster transdisciplinary and philosophical reflection on the many contemporary interactions between computer science, logic, and law (as well as the theoretical foundations of these interactions). Three previous editions have been organized: 

- 2023: Université Jean Moulin Lyon 3, Faculté de Philosophie, IRPhiL, 08-09/11/2023, coord. : Jean-Baptiste Joinet et Hugo Cadière :["Les interfaces Logique, Informatique, Droit : Horizons Philosophiques“(https://facdephilo.univ-lyon3.fr/colloque-les-interfaces-logique-informatique-droit-horizons-philosophiques)];
- 2024: Université Paris-Est Créteil, Law Faculty, 28-29/11/2024, coord. Luc Pelissier : [[“Vérité et Obligation”](/files/2026/NORMES-2024.pdf)]
- 2025: Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne (IHPST), 6-7/10/2025, coord. Alberto Naibo (Univ. Paris 1, IHPST) et Pierre-Yves Quiviger (Univ. Paris 1, ISJPS),

The scientific committee for the Bologna workshop  has proposed the following general theme for the 2026 meeting: **“Norming normes/Normare le norme/Normer les normes”**. Behind this title—which is quite abstract and may appear somewhat narrow or restrictive—the goal is to stimulate reflection through diverse and open perspectives on the interfaces between computer science, logic, and law. This encompasses argumentation and formalization, the use or role of logic and computer science (broadly defined, including AI) in legal theory, and even computer science as a subject of law itself.

## Practical information

The conference will take place in November&nbsp;2026 over three half-days: 
- Tuesday afternoon, November&nbsp;4;
- Wednesday morning, November&nbsp;5; 
- Wednesday afternoon, November&nbsp;5.

It will be held in the **Sala Manfredi** at the [Academy of Sciences of the Institute of Bologna](https://site.unibo.it/accademiascienzebologna/it) (Accademia delle Scienze dell'Istituto di Bologna), located at [Via Zamboni 31, 40126 Bologna](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/2018955).
