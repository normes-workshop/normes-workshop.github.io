Created four years ago, the **NORMES group** (successor to a previous multidisciplinary research collective, LIGC, focused on logic, computer science, and their interactions with other fields) has aimed to foster transdisciplinary reflection on the many contemporary interactions between computer science, logic, and law (as well as the theoretical foundations of these interactions). 

## Editions

- [2026, Bologna](/2026/)
- 2024, Créteil
- [2023, Lyon](https://facdephilo.univ-lyon3.fr/colloque-les-interfaces-logique-informatique-droit-horizons-philosophiques)
